# Nachweis (konforme-ki.de) — Astro static site

German-only, multi-page site for **Nachweis**: an EU-only AI governance/inference product whose product is the exportable, hash-chained audit trail (Nachweis), not the tokens. Target audience is broad — any EU company with a Datenschutzbeauftragter or Betriebsrat blocking an AI rollout (Art. 28 DSGVO, US CLOUD Act/GeschGehG, §87 Abs. 1 Nr. 6 BetrVG) — with Berufsgeheimnisträger under § 203 StGB (Steuerberatung, Rechtsanwaltschaft, Arzt-/Zahnarztpraxen, Notariate, Psychotherapie, Pflegedienste) kept as a named, still-served niche rather than the whole pitch. **The primary homepage offer is Sidecar** — a governance/attestation layer over a customer's *existing* inference (Azure OpenAI, Bedrock, own cloud), zero migration, zero GPU capex; the fully hosted Compliance tier is the escalation path for cases a customer's own cloud can't cover, not the opening ask. See `MARKETING.md`'s "One vertical, not three" and "Infra & architecture critique" sections before reverting either of these — they're deliberate, not drift. Astro 7 + Tailwind v4 (Vite plugin, no `tailwind.config`), static output, deployed to GitHub Pages.

Business context and the funnel design live in `MARKETING.md` at the repo root — read it before making content changes.

## The shared plumbing layer: `@justinguese/astro-kit`

This site is migrated onto `@justinguese/astro-kit` v0.4.0 (npm, `~/code/websites/datafortress-astro-kit`), the shared "invisible correctness layer" also used by pruefanfrage.de: consent-gated analytics, first-touch attribution, SEO head, funnel/conversion tracking, prose CSS, role tokens, and a growing set of section blocks — `HeroBlock`, `ScarcityBlock`, `PricingCards`, `FaqBlock`, `FormBlock`, `SectionHeader`, `CtaBand`, `ProofBlock`, `ArticleLayout`, `ArticleGrid`, `Breadcrumbs`, `ReadingProgress`. **Read the kit's own `AGENTS.md` before touching consent, tracking, forms, the `@theme` tokens in `global.css`, or any of these blocks** — it documents the paired contracts (`CookieBanner`↔`Consent`, `Attribution`↔`TrackingFields`, `FormBlock`↔`ConversionTracking`), the migration playbook (visible-text diffing technique), and the hard rules (no color literals outside the site's own `@theme` block, never add `@source` for the kit).

A caret range on the kit's `0.x` version locks the MINOR — `^0.4.0` will not accept `0.5.0`. Bumping to a new kit minor needs `package.json` edited by hand, `npm install`, then a rebuild + `astro check` + visible-text diff, same as any migration step. `CompareBlock` (v0.4.0) has no adoption site here — nothing on this site compares against competitors the way `PricingMatrix`/`PricingCards` compare tiers against each other.

**`SectionHeader`'s `title`/`lead` are plain-string props, not slots** (unlike `HeroBlock`'s `headline`). Any section whose heading or lead paragraph needs inline markup — a colored `<span>`, a `<strong>`, a `<code>` — cannot use it without losing that emphasis. `#nachweis` and `#checkliste` on the homepage, and the h1 on `/integrationen` and `/playground`, are left as hand-written headers for exactly this reason. Don't "finish the sweep" on those without re-solving that problem first.

What stayed local, deliberately, and why (don't "finish the migration" by moving these — they were evaluated and kept):
- **`LeiterTabelle.astro`** (the homepage offer ladder) — the kit's `PricingCards`/`Tier` type has no field for `eignung` (audience-fit sentence per tier) or `nummer` (ledger numbering), and the `ANKER` price-anchor panel is fused into the same component.
- **`ZugangForm.astro`** — its `_next` redirect value and submit-button label both change client-side after reading `?tarif=`, which the kit's `FormBlock` has no hook for (single computed `_next`, static `submitLabel`). It still uses the kit's `TrackingFields` directly.
- **`Kette.astro`** (the hash-chain rail), **`RekordKarte.astro`**, **`Playground.astro`** — these are the site's own identity, not plumbing; the kit's `AGENTS.md` explicitly calls out the hash-chain as a "stays in its site" example. `Kette.astro`'s `.kette-strich` also duplicates the kit's `ReadingProgress` — kept local because it's already branded into the `Kette` identity, not swapped.
- **`Belege.astro`**'s stat cards — adopted the kit's `ProofBlock` (stats-only, no quotes — see below), but overrode its default dark `--color-premium-bright` card styling via a local `.belege-hell` class in `global.css`, since this site reserves premium exclusively for Säule 3 and the section sits on a light background. The override, not the block itself, is what's local here.
- **`src/pages/unterlagen/[slug].astro`** — not moved onto `ArticleLayout`. It's a printable reference document (own `.druck-kopf` print header, "Als PDF drucken" button), not a long-form article; `ArticleLayout`'s breadcrumbs/TOC/related furniture don't fit that use case the way they do `ratgeber/[slug].astro`.

`ProofBlock`'s `quotes` are unused on purpose: `Belege.astro` passes `stats` only. The section's own headline — "Wir zitieren keine Kunden, die wir noch nicht haben" — is a deliberate no-quotes stance (see the § 5 UWG note in `belege.ts`), not a placeholder waiting to be filled in. If real testimonials are ever added, use `ProofBlock`'s `quotes` prop with `unverified` unset (only while genuinely unverified) — never invent one to fill the block's shape.

## Structure

- `src/pages/index.astro` — homepage, 12 chained sections ordered by **funnel logic, not product logic**: `hero` → `problem` → `sidecar` → `playground` → `nachweis` → `unterlagen` → `checkliste` → `integrationen` → `angebote` → `zugang` → `faq` → `abschluss`. Keep the disclaimers and off-page links **after** `zugang` — in front of the form they cost conversions. Uses the kit's `HeroBlock`, `ScarcityBlock`, `FaqBlock`, `CtaBand`, and `SectionHeader` on every section opener except `#nachweis`/`#checkliste` (inline `<span>` in the heading — see above).
- `src/pages/playground.astro` — full scripted playground (see below). Its h1's lead has an inline `<strong>`, so the header stays hand-written.
- `src/pages/integrationen.astro`, `eu-ki-recht.astro`, `preise.astro` — secondary content pages. `/preise` uses the kit's `PricingCards` + `SectionHeader`. `/integrationen`'s h1 stays hand-written (inline `<code>` in the lead); its other two section headers use `SectionHeader`.
- `src/pages/unterlagen/index.astro` + `[slug].astro` — the ungated lead magnet, rendering `src/content/unterlagen/*.md` (its own local schema — no kit equivalent). The index page's listing uses the kit's `ArticleGrid` + `SectionHeader`; `[slug].astro` keeps its own print-focused template (not `ArticleLayout` — see below).
- `src/pages/ratgeber/index.astro` + `[slug].astro` — SEO articles from `src/content/ratgeber/*.md`, grouped by `category`. The index page's listing uses `ArticleGrid` + `SectionHeader`. `[slug].astro` uses the kit's `ArticleLayout` (breadcrumbs, sticky TOC, header, body, disclaimer slot, `ArtikelUpsell.astro` in the cta slot, related-articles via `ArticleGrid`) — `progress={false}` since the site's own `kette-strich` bar already covers that. No article currently sets `faq` frontmatter, so `ArticleLayout`'s FAQ section never renders; don't invent Q&A to fill it (see "Articles must not invent facts" below).
- `src/pages/[slug].astro` — single dynamic route rendering `src/content/legal/*.md` (impressum, datenschutz, agb, rdg-hinweis). Don't recreate the old one-file-per-page wrappers.
- `src/pages/kontakt.astro` — secondary contact form, reached via `?interesse=onboarding|sidecar|compliance`. Uses the kit's `FormBlock` + `ConversionTracking`.
- `src/layouts/Site.astro` — shared shell built on the kit's `SeoHead`, `Consent`, `CookieBanner`, `StickyCta`, `Attribution` and `FunnelTracking` (see the kit's `AGENTS.md` for what each does). The sticky mobile bar targets `/?tarif=compliance#zugang`, not `/playground`. Also owns the `kette-strich` mobile progress line and the nav `<details>` menu, which stay site-local.
- `src/config/site.ts` — the one place brand name, address, GA4/Meta Pixel IDs and the Formspree id live (`defineSiteConfig`). All public by design; never put a secret here.
- `src/lib/kette.ts` — build-time hash chain for the `Kette` (chain rail) signature element. Real sha256, not decorative.
- `src/lib/playground/{types,script,transport}.ts` — the playground's swappable transport. `scriptedTransport` (default, no backend) vs. `httpTransport` (used automatically once `PUBLIC_GATEWAY_URL` is set). Don't hardcode which one runs — always go through `getTransport()`.
- `src/lib/preise.ts` — single source of truth for pricing. Two distinct things: `LEITER` + `ANKER` (the **offer ladder** — €0 checklist / €39 dossier / €290 compliance, plus the €1,900–9,000 anchor; rendered by `LeiterTabelle.astro` on the homepage, kept local) and `NUTZUNGSMODELLE`/`SERVICE_TARIFE` (**billing models**, rendered by the kit's `PricingCards` on `/preise` via the `tarifAlsTier()` adapter in this file). The ladder is the buying decision; the token tiers are a billing detail — don't merge them back into one table on the homepage. Field names stay German (`tarifId`, `preis`, `einheit`, …) throughout — `ZugangForm.astro` loops over `LEITER`+`ANKER`+`NUTZUNGSMODELLE` together assuming one shared shape, so don't rename fields on just one of them.
- `src/lib/belege.ts` — social proof. `Belege.astro` renders `SIEGEL`, four factual claims (not testimonials). **If this ever grows a quote/testimonial list, invented ones are unlawful in Germany (§ 5 UWG)** — use only released quotes.
- `src/lib/kontingent.ts` — the scarcity numbers (monthly access-key quota), rendered via the kit's `ScarcityBlock` (wrapped in a local `.kontingent-akzent` class — see `global.css` — so it renders in this site's accent color rather than the kit's default `--color-signal`, which on this site means exclusively "fail closed"). Real, because keys genuinely are issued manually; bump `vergeben` after each round. Never turn this into an auto-incrementing fake counter.

## Conventions

- **No i18n.** The site is German-only (`lang="de"` hardcoded). Don't reintroduce locale routing.
- **Lead capture is Formspree-only**, no custom backend, no payment provider. One endpoint now (`site.formspreeId` in `src/config/site.ts`, public by design — no env var), separated by the kit's `TrackingFields variant=` hidden field instead of three separate form ids: `zugang` (waitlist + €39 dossier orders), `kontakt`, `allgemein`/`checkliste`/`infografik` for the PDF checklist gate (used on both `/` and `/eu-ki-recht` via `quelle`/`id` props so the two instances stay distinguishable in the id-prefixed DOM, though the Formspree `form` field itself stays the constant `infografik` for both). Success handling is the kit's `ConversionTracking` (analytics + URL cleanup) plus a small per-form local script (reveal/hide the two DOM panels) — bypassable by hand-editing the URL; accepted limitation of the static-site approach.
- **Every offer CTA must be attributable.** Content CTAs carry `data-cta="<tarifId>"`, picked up by the kit's `FunnelTracking` (fires `cta_click` with `{cta: label}` — one event name, the label as a parameter, never `${tarif}_cta_click`). Links additionally go through `zugangHref(tarifId)` → `/?tarif=<id>#zugang`; `ZugangForm.astro` reads the param into hidden `tarif`/`tarif_klartext` fields and adapts its submit label. Without this, all CTAs land in the Formspree inbox indistinguishable from each other. New offers get a `tarifId` in `preise.ts` — never a bare `/#zugang` link.
- **DataFortress.cloud GmbH is the face of the product, not an EU third party.** `STANDARD_SAEULE` in `src/lib/saeulen.ts` (= Säule 2, own infrastructure in Frankfurt, no external subprocessor) drives the playground's preselected pillar and the example records on the homepage. Never put a third-party provider name (Mistral, IONOS, …) in the hero or the primary `RekordKarte` — a foreign vendor name in the showcase record raises exactly the question the product exists to answer. Säule 1 stays selectable and is named in the pricing and FAQ.
- **No hardcoded dates in anything time-sensitive.** The site is statically built and not rebuilt per visit, so a compiled-in month or date is wrong within weeks. Scarcity copy uses relative wording only ("diesen Monat", "pro Monat") — see `src/lib/kontingent.ts`. The playground's recorded records get their `zeitstempel` stamped with `new Date()` at playback (`mitJetztStempel` in `transport.ts`). If you ever need a concrete date on screen, generate it client-side. `Stand:`/`updated:` version dates on legal texts, Unterlagen and Ratgeber articles are the deliberate exception — they document when content was last reviewed and must stay.
- **The playground never fakes being live inference without saying so.** Every instance of `<Playground />` must show the "Demo · aufgezeichnete Antworten, keine Live-Inferenz" label. Don't invent latency/throughput numbers.
- **Consent Mode v2 is load-bearing, not decorative.** GA4/Meta Pixel must stay `denied` by default and only fire after `cookie-consent-updated` (accepted) — handled by the kit's `Consent`/`CookieBanner` pair now. A compliance product that tracks before consent is a credibility bug, not a minor one — don't regress `Site.astro`'s consent gating when touching analytics. Verify with a real browser in an isolated context (fresh visitor, accept, reload-after-accept, decline-then-reload, and the footer's `data-consent-reopen` button), never by reading the code — see the kit's `AGENTS.md` for the exact checks.
- **GA4/Meta Pixel/Formspree IDs live in `src/config/site.ts`** (public by design — they compile into static HTML). `PUBLIC_GATEWAY_URL` is the only env var left (see `.env.example`), injected via `env:` in `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` from a repo secret.
- **SEO articles (`src/content/ratgeber/`) follow a fixed recipe**: minimum 1200 words, 9-13 short `##` sections, no `#` H1 (the template renders `title`), and **no closing CTA in the markdown** — `ArtikelUpsell.astro` renders it. Every article ends on a bridge paragraph about the gap between being compliant and being able to prove it. `metaTitle` is keyword-first and may differ from `title`; `description` stays at 150-158 characters. `updated` is a real ISO date (`2026-08-07`, quoted or not — the kit's schema accepts both YAML spellings) — it feeds JSON-LD `dateModified` and `<meta property="article:modified_time">` directly, so don't put a German-formatted string there; the display format on the page comes from `formatDeDatum()` in `src/lib/format.ts`.
- **Articles must not invent facts.** No customer names, testimonials, statistics, percentages, court rulings, Bußgeld amounts, or benchmark numbers. **Regulatory dates come only from `eu-ki-recht.astro`** — it is the single source of truth for the AI Act timeline (including the Digital Omnibus shift of Annex III to 2. Dezember 2027) and the DPF status. Never recall a date from memory. Discuss competitors as categories and patterns, never with specific factual accusations.
- **Markdown tables scroll themselves.** `.article-body` structure comes from the kit's `styles/prose.css`, but its default table is `width: 100%` with a `min-width` per cell — that reintroduces page-level horizontal scroll on this site (a real regression that happened once already), so `global.css` overrides `.article-body table`/`td` back to `width: max-content` / `max-width: 100%` / no `min-width`, right after the kit import. Never remove that override or add `min-width` back.
- **Legal and regulatory accuracy matters here.** `datenschutz.md`, `agb.md`, `rdg-hinweis.md`, everything in `src/content/unterlagen/`, and `eu-ki-recht.astro` are legal/regulatory content, not marketing copy — flag material changes rather than silently editing. Regulatory dates (AI Act, DSGVO) must be verified against a current source before writing, not recalled from memory; see the `Stand:` date on `eu-ki-recht.astro`.
- **Never claim an unshipped integration, customer, or metric.** `KanalKarte` status badges must reflect reality — default to `in-vorbereitung`/`auf-anfrage`, not `verfuegbar`, unless it's actually live.
- **The upstream list is fixed and EU-only**: Scaleway, IONOS AI Model Hub, Mistral AI, OVHcloud AI Endpoints. Never reference OpenRouter or a US provider as an upstream — that's the whole premise of the product (see `MARKETING.md`).

## Deploy

GitHub Actions → GitHub Pages (`.github/workflows/deploy.yml`, `withastro/action@v6`), triggered on push to `main`. `PUBLIC_GATEWAY_URL` is injected from a repo secret at build time; everything else (Formspree, GA4, Meta Pixel) is committed in `src/config/site.ts`, not a deploy-time secret.
