# Nachweis (konforme-ki.de) — Astro static site

German-only, multi-page site for **Nachweis**: an EU-only AI inference gateway whose product is the exportable, hash-chained audit trail (Nachweis), not the tokens. Target audience: Berufsgeheimnisträger under § 203 StGB — Steuerberatung, Rechtsanwaltschaft, Arzt-/Zahnarztpraxen, Notariate, Psychotherapie, Pflegedienste. Astro 7 + Tailwind v4 (Vite plugin, no `tailwind.config`), static output, deployed to GitHub Pages.

Business context and the funnel design live in `MARKETING.md` at the repo root — read it before making content changes.

## Structure

- `src/pages/index.astro` — homepage, 16 chained sections ordered by **funnel logic, not product logic**: pain (`problem`) → free rung (`checkliste`) → solution (`nachweis`/`playground`/`saeulen`) → proof (`belege`) → offer (`angebote`) → action (`zugang`) → trust and caveats (`unterlagen`, `eu-ki-recht`, `nicht`, `faq`) → closing CTA (`abschluss`). Keep `nicht` (the disclaimers) and the off-page links **after** `zugang` — in front of the form they cost conversions.
- `src/pages/playground.astro` — full scripted playground (see below).
- `src/pages/integrationen.astro`, `eu-ki-recht.astro`, `preise.astro` — secondary content pages.
- `src/pages/unterlagen/index.astro` + `[slug].astro` — the ungated lead magnet, rendering `src/content/unterlagen/*.md`.
- `src/pages/ratgeber/index.astro` + `[slug].astro` — SEO articles from `src/content/ratgeber/*.md`, grouped by `kategorie`. Each article renders a breadcrumb, an auto-generated table of contents (H2 only), `Article` + `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD, `ArtikelUpsell.astro`, related-article links from `verwandt`, and a legal disclaimer.
- `src/pages/[slug].astro` — single dynamic route rendering `src/content/legal/*.md` (impressum, datenschutz, agb, rdg-hinweis). Don't recreate the old one-file-per-page wrappers.
- `src/pages/kontakt.astro` — secondary contact form, reached via `?interesse=onboarding|sidecar|compliance`.
- `src/layouts/Site.astro` — shared shell: head/meta/OG, Consent-Mode-v2-gated GA4 + Meta Pixel, nav, footer, sticky mobile CTA (→ `/playground`), the `kette-strich` mobile progress line.
- `src/lib/kette.ts` — build-time hash chain for the `Kette` (chain rail) signature element. Real sha256, not decorative.
- `src/lib/playground/{types,script,transport}.ts` — the playground's swappable transport. `scriptedTransport` (default, no backend) vs. `httpTransport` (used automatically once `PUBLIC_GATEWAY_URL` is set). Don't hardcode which one runs — always go through `getTransport()`.
- `src/lib/preise.ts` — single source of truth for pricing. Two distinct things: `LEITER` + `ANKER` (the **offer ladder** — €0 checklist / €39 dossier / €290 compliance, plus the €1,900–9,000 anchor; rendered by `LeiterTabelle.astro` on the homepage) and `NUTZUNGSMODELLE`/`SERVICE_TARIFE` (**billing models**, rendered by `PreisTabelle.astro` on `/preise`). The ladder is the buying decision; the token tiers are a billing detail — don't merge them back into one table on the homepage.
- `src/lib/belege.ts` — social proof. Every `STIMMEN` entry carries `platzhalter`; while any is `true`, `Belege.astro` renders a loud placeholder warning. **Invented testimonials are unlawful in Germany (§ 5 UWG)** — replace with released quotes or delete the section before launch, never just flip the flag.
- `src/lib/kontingent.ts` — the scarcity numbers (monthly access-key quota). Real, because keys genuinely are issued manually; bump `vergeben` after each round. Never turn this into an auto-incrementing fake counter.

## Conventions

- **No i18n.** The site is German-only (`lang="de"` hardcoded). Don't reintroduce locale routing.
- **Lead capture is Formspree-only**, no custom backend, no payment provider. Three endpoints: `PUBLIC_FORMSPREE_ZUGANG_ID` (homepage waitlist _and_ €39 dossier orders), `PUBLIC_FORMSPREE_KONTAKT_ID` (kontakt.astro), and `PUBLIC_FORMSPREE_INFOGRAFIK_ID` (the PDF checklist gate, used on both `/` and `/eu-ki-recht` — pass `next`/`quelle`/`id` props so the two instances stay distinguishable). Gating (success banners, and — for the PDF — revealing the download link) is a client-side query-param check after the Formspree redirect — bypassable by hand-editing the URL; accepted limitation of the static-site approach.
- **Every offer CTA must be attributable.** Links go through `zugangHref(tarifId)` → `/?tarif=<id>#zugang`; `ZugangForm.astro` reads the param into hidden `tarif`/`tarif_klartext` fields and adapts its submit label. Without this, all CTAs land in the Formspree inbox indistinguishable from each other. New offers get a `tarifId` in `preise.ts` — never a bare `/#zugang` link.
- **DataFortress.cloud GmbH is the face of the product, not an EU third party.** `STANDARD_SAEULE` in `src/lib/saeulen.ts` (= Säule 2, own infrastructure in Frankfurt, no external subprocessor) drives the playground's preselected pillar and the example records on the homepage. Never put a third-party provider name (Mistral, IONOS, …) in the hero or the primary `RekordKarte` — a foreign vendor name in the showcase record raises exactly the question the product exists to answer. Säule 1 stays selectable and is named in the pricing and FAQ.
- **No hardcoded dates in anything time-sensitive.** The site is statically built and not rebuilt per visit, so a compiled-in month or date is wrong within weeks. Scarcity copy uses relative wording only ("diesen Monat", "pro Monat") — see `src/lib/kontingent.ts`. The playground's recorded records get their `zeitstempel` stamped with `new Date()` at playback (`mitJetztStempel` in `transport.ts`). If you ever need a concrete date on screen, generate it client-side. `Stand:`/`updated:` version dates on legal texts, Unterlagen and Ratgeber articles are the deliberate exception — they document when content was last reviewed and must stay.
- **The playground never fakes being live inference without saying so.** Every instance of `<Playground />` must show the "Demo · aufgezeichnete Antworten, keine Live-Inferenz" label. Don't invent latency/throughput numbers.
- **Consent Mode v2 is load-bearing, not decorative.** GA4/Meta Pixel must stay `denied` by default and only fire after `cookie-consent-updated` (accepted). A compliance product that tracks before consent is a credibility bug, not a minor one — don't regress `Site.astro`'s consent gating when touching analytics.
- **Analytics/Formspree IDs are all env-driven** (`PUBLIC_GA4_ID`, `PUBLIC_META_PIXEL_ID`, `PUBLIC_FORMSPREE_ZUGANG_ID`, `PUBLIC_FORMSPREE_KONTAKT_ID`, `PUBLIC_FORMSPREE_INFOGRAFIK_ID`, `PUBLIC_GATEWAY_URL` — see `.env.example`). Injected via `env:` in `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` from repo secrets. Empty GA4/Pixel IDs render no snippet at all (no placeholder IDs baked in).
- **SEO articles (`src/content/ratgeber/`) follow a fixed recipe**: minimum 1200 words, 9-13 short `##` sections, no `#` H1 (the template renders `title`), and **no closing CTA in the markdown** — `ArtikelUpsell.astro` renders it. Every article ends on a bridge paragraph about the gap between being compliant and being able to prove it. `metaTitle` is keyword-first and may differ from `title`; `beschreibung` stays at 150-158 characters.
- **Articles must not invent facts.** No customer names, testimonials, statistics, percentages, court rulings, Bußgeld amounts, or benchmark numbers. **Regulatory dates come only from `eu-ki-recht.astro`** — it is the single source of truth for the AI Act timeline (including the Digital Omnibus shift of Annex III to 2. Dezember 2027) and the DPF status. Never recall a date from memory. Discuss competitors as categories and patterns, never with specific factual accusations.
- **Markdown tables scroll themselves.** Astro 7's default Markdown processor takes no rehype plugins without swapping the whole pipeline, so there is no wrapper element — `.article-body table` is `display: block` + `width: max-content` + `max-width: 100%` instead. Never add `min-width` to it: `min-width` beats `max-width` and the page starts scrolling horizontally again.
- **Legal and regulatory accuracy matters here.** `datenschutz.md`, `agb.md`, `rdg-hinweis.md`, everything in `src/content/unterlagen/`, and `eu-ki-recht.astro` are legal/regulatory content, not marketing copy — flag material changes rather than silently editing. Regulatory dates (AI Act, DSGVO) must be verified against a current source before writing, not recalled from memory; see the `Stand:` date on `eu-ki-recht.astro`.
- **Never claim an unshipped integration, customer, or metric.** `KanalKarte` status badges must reflect reality — default to `in-vorbereitung`/`auf-anfrage`, not `verfuegbar`, unless it's actually live.
- **The upstream list is fixed and EU-only**: Scaleway, IONOS AI Model Hub, Mistral AI, OVHcloud AI Endpoints. Never reference OpenRouter or a US provider as an upstream — that's the whole premise of the product (see `MARKETING.md`).

## Deploy

GitHub Actions → GitHub Pages (`.github/workflows/deploy.yml`, `withastro/action@v6`), triggered on push to `main`. Env vars for Formspree/analytics/gateway are injected from repo secrets at build time — set them in GitHub repo settings before the first real deploy.
