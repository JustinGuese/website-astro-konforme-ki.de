# Summary: konforme-ki.de

konforme-ki.de provides AI inference hosted on dedicated bare-metal hardware in Nürnberg, Germany, backed by a legally signed § 203 StGB confidentiality agreement (Verschwiegenheitsvereinbarung) and immutable SHA-256 hash-chained audit trails. It is designed specifically for German lawyers, tax advisors, notaries, and medical professionals handling confidential client data.

**Verdict: Profitable** (84% contribution margin yielding $245.00 per client-month, break-even at 1 client/day against a 10/day capacity, and Year 1 projected operating profit of $224,310).

## Board & Panel Alignment
- **Agreement:** Both the board and consumer panel identified the critical unserved wedge: generic cloud providers (IONOS, STACKIT) cannot and will not sign personal criminal liability addenda under § 203 StGB.
- **Disagreement / Nuance:** The board raised valid concerns regarding infrastructure leakage and legal positioning under RDG; the marketing plan addresses this with strict non-advice disclaimers and verified Nürnberg bare-metal routing.

## Biggest Risk & Mitigation
- **Risk:** Infrastructure leakage or third-party subprocessor routing (e.g. US cloud edges) undermining the zero-US-involvement promise.
- **Mitigation:** Dedicated bare-metal Nürnberg hosting with TLS termination and verifiable open-source cryptographic audit logging (SHA-256 hash chain).
