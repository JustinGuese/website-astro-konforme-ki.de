## The idea

- What it is: A German LLM compliance & inference platform providing a signed § 203 StGB confidentiality agreement (Verschwiegenheitsvereinbarung) and cryptographically hash-chained audit trails for German professional secret holders and enterprises.
- Who it is for: Berufsgeheimnisträger under § 203 StGB (lawyers, tax advisors, medical practices, notaries) in Germany with strict regulatory requirements, plus general B2B teams via Router and Sidecar.
- What it sells, at what price: Free tier (5M tokens/month), €290/month compliance tier, €1,900–9,000 once for custom integration / compliance checks.
- Where and how: Online at konforme-ki.de, delivered via custom German infrastructure in Nürnberg (Deutschland-Inferenz), OpenAI-compatible API gateway (Router), or customer cloud sidecar.
- Budget and constraints: Bootstrapped German GmbH (DataFortress.cloud GmbH), single GPU / Nürnberg hardware initially, strict adherence to legal limitations (RDG, no false absolute exclusivity claims against major players like IONOS/STACKIT/T-Systems).
