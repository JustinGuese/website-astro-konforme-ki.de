# Pricing Strategy: konforme-ki.de

## 1. The Price and Why
- **Core SaaS Compliance Tier:** **€290 per month** (plus usage or bundled free tier tokens).
- **Why it sits here:** 
  - Positioned well below high-end enterprise sovereign clusters (€2,000–10,000/mo from T-Systems or Aleph Alpha) which are inaccessible to solo practitioners and mid-sized law/tax firms.
  - Positioned above generic developer APIs or consumer ChatGPT Plus (€20/mo) because it includes the legal and compliance overhead: signed § 203 StGB confidentiality agreements (Verschwiegenheitsvereinbarung, Anlage 1) and SHA-256 cryptographic hash-chained audit trails.
  - For a law firm or medical practice billing €150–€350+ per hour, saving 2 hours of administrative document drafting per month or eliminating compliance exposure entirely makes a €290/mo subscription an immediate net-positive return on investment.

## 2. The Product Value Ladder
The buying journey follows a clear, low-friction value ladder:
1. **Rung 1 (€0 — Freigabe-Checkliste):** 1-page PDF checklist covering AI Act, DSGVO, and § 203 StGB requirements to take to the internal Data Protection Officer (DSB). Removes initial friction and captures high-intent email leads.
2. **Rung 2 (€39 — §203-Freigabedossier):** One-time purchase providing the complete unterschriftsreif document set (Art. 28 AVV, § 203 Abs. 3 StGB addendum, Art. 30 VVT entry, TOM description). *Fully credited toward any subsequent compliance subscription.*
3. **Rung 3 (€290/month — Compliance Subscription):** The core recurring offer. Includes ongoing cryptographically hash-chained audit trails, automated Art. 30 VVT logging, subprocessor change notifications, and signed compliance paperwork.
4. **Rung 4 (€1,900 – €9,000 — Enterprise / Custom Integration):** Dedicated Nürnberg hardware instances, custom Sidecar deployment in existing Azure/AWS environments, and tailored SLA / legal review for large practices and clinics.

## 3. Opening Launch Offer
- **Launch Special for Early Adopters:** First month of the Compliance tier (€290) included free with any annual commitment, or a 14-day fully provisioned trial with 10 million tokens and signed compliance paperwork generated instantly.
- **Rule:** No permanent discounting or training buyers to wait for sales. The value is anchored in legal certainty and uncompromised sovereign infrastructure.

## 4. What to Test with Real Buyers
Because the simulated consumer panel could not run automated subagent rounds due to platform API rate limits, pricing assumptions will be validated via live landing page A/B tests:
- **Test A vs. Test B:** Presenting the Compliance tier at **€249/month** vs. **€290/month** on split traffic (`/preise`) to measure conversion lift vs. perceived enterprise trust. (Professional service buyers often equate lower pricing with lower legal robustness; €290 signals serious legal backing).
- **Pre-sale validation:** Tracking conversion rates on `dossierHref` (€39) as a direct proxy for willingness-to-pay for compliance documentation.

## 5. Top Price & Trust Objections (To Answer in Marketing & Sales)
1. **"Is €290/month too high when OpenAI API tokens cost pennies?"**
   - *Answer:* You are not paying for raw tokens; you are paying for the legal shield of a signed § 203 StGB confidentiality agreement and cryptographic audit trails that protect against criminal prosecution.
2. **"Does a signed contract from a GmbH actually protect me against criminal liability under § 203 StGB?"**
   - *Answer:* Yes, structured exactly as an auxiliary processor arrangement under § 203 Abs. 3 StGB with explicit executive accountability and dedicated Nürnberg physical hardware.
3. **"Why not just use free local open-source models (Ollama)?"**
   - *Answer:* Local hardware lacks the cryptographic audit trail, immutable hash chaining, and signed legal paperwork required by professional chambers and liability insurers upon audit.
