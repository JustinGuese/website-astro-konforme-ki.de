# Product lens

Score: 8/10 | Vote: FUND

## What is strong
- **Razor-sharp focus:** The September 15 repositioning explicitly targets § 203 StGB Berufsgeheimnisträger in the hero, moving generic routers and sidecars behind the primary access flow.
- **Craft and transparency:** Explicit `#grenzen` section, no fake testimonials ("Wir zitieren keine Kunden, die wir noch nicht haben"), and clear technical explanations (RekordKarte, hash chaining).
- **Simplicity of the core action:** The onboarding and quiz (§203 compliance check) guide users directly to the right product and document checklist.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Infrastructure leakage:** Technical mismatches (e.g., Cloudflare tunnels or non-Nürnberg routing) undermining the core promise of zero US involvement.
2. **Cluttering the interface:** Reintroducing complex multi-product matrices into the first viewport.
3. **Over-promising on legal certainty:** Violating the RDG (Legal Services Act) by crossing from technical/contractual tooling into giving formal legal advice.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Does the production infrastructure (Nürnberg hardware, TLS termination) 100% match the claims made in the RekordKarte today?
- Is the questionnaire experience under 60 seconds without friction?
- How do we ensure legal disclaimers remain visible without killing conversion momentum?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
- Maintain ruthless simplicity on the landing page and ensure every technical claim in the audit trail RekordKarte is verifiable in production.
