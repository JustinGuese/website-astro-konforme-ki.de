# Offers lens

Score: 7/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **Massive, urgent pain:** Berufsgeheimnisträger (§ 203 StGB: lawyers, tax advisors, doctors, notaries) face personal criminal liability for data disclosure, making compliance not a nice-to-have but a legal imperative.
- **Value stack & ladder:** Clear entry point (Free tier with 5M tokens / €0 checklist), mid-tier (§203-Dossier / €290/mo compliance subscription), and high-value integration packages (€1,900–9,000).
- **Risk reversal:** Transparent `#grenzen` (telling users when *not* to buy) builds immense trust and increases conversion quality.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Credibility gap:** If buyers doubt that a small GmbH's § 203 addendum will protect them in a chamber audit or court proceeding.
2. **Friction in issuance:** Manual key generation and signature workflows bottlenecking rapid adoption if not streamlined.
3. **Value confusion:** Mixing generic developer inference (Router/Sidecar) into the primary secret-holder pitch, diluting the "Schweigepflicht" message.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- How long does it take from clicking "Kostenlos testen" to having a legally signed agreement and a working API key?
- What percentage of sign-ups convert from the 5M free tier to the €290/mo compliance subscription?
- What specific legal review backs the § 203 textform addendum?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
- Must strictly isolate the § 203 Deutschland-Inferenz offer from generic developer routers in all primary touchpoints so buyers instantly understand the legal guarantee.
