# Marketing Strategy: konforme-ki.de

## 1. Positioning

### One-Line Positioning
For German lawyers, tax advisors, notaries, and medical practitioners who want to use AI for document drafting and case prep without violating professional secrecy (§ 203 StGB), **konforme-ki.de** is the sovereign AI inference and compliance platform that delivers signed legal agreements and cryptographic audit trails, unlike US cloud APIs that refuse personal criminal liability addendums.

### Three Positioning Versions
1. **The Legal Compliance Angle:** "The only AI platform built specifically for § 203 StGB Berufsgeheimnisträger, combining dedicated Nürnberg hardware with signed confidentiality addendums and immutable audit trails." *(Recommended)*
2. **The Sovereign Infrastructure Angle:** "German-hosted AI inference with zero US subprocessors, complete data residency, and automated Art. 30 VVT logging for regulated professional practices."
3. **The Frictionless Workflow Angle:** "Plug-and-play OpenAI-compatible AI endpoints that your team can use instantly in existing tools, backed by unassailable compliance documentation."

### Evidence from Dossier
The board memos (`board.md`), competitor analysis (`competitors.md`), and customer profiles (`customer.json`) confirm that general sovereign cloud providers (IONOS, STACKIT) win on generic ISO/C5 IT compliance but explicitly refuse personal criminal liability addenda under § 203 StGB. Positioning squarely on § 203 StGB compliance and signed legal wrappers captures the exact defensible gap.

## 2. Channels
1. **Meta Ads (Broad Targeting + Instant Forms):** 
   - *Why:* Reaches firm partners and practice directors directly on mobile and desktop without relying on restrictive B2B keyword stacks that miss decision-makers. Instant form for the free Freigabe-Checkliste converts without friction before cookie-consent drop-off.
   - *Cost:* €40–€100/day starting budget.
   - *Measurement:* Instant form submissions (`FreeDownload`), tracked via CAPI + Funnel API.
2. **Professional Media & Chamber Newsletters (BRAK-Mitteilungen, DStR, Kanzlei-Newsletter):**
   - *Why:* Target audience regularly reads professional trade journals and chamber communications regarding data protection and software compliance.
   - *Cost:* €1,500 – €3,000 per sponsored feature or banner placement.
   - *Measurement:* UTM-tagged landing page visits and `einstufung` quiz completions.
3. **Direct Professional Referrals & DSB Networks:**
   - *Why:* Data Protection Officers (DSBs) advising multiple law and medical practices act as trusted multipliers.
   - *Cost:* Zero direct ad spend; referral incentives on the compliance subscription.
   - *Measurement:* Referral attribution codes in `zugang` forms.

*What we are NOT doing:* General Google Search keyword bidding on high-cost generic AI terms (competing with Microsoft and OpenAI on "ChatGPT API" is capital-inefficient); programmatic display ads (low trust among legal/medical professionals).

## 3. The 30-Day Launch Campaign Calendar (Day -14 to Day +16)

### Ten Hooks (Answering Panel Objections & Buyer Motivations)
1. *"Der Kollege tippt Mandantendaten in ChatGPT — ohne zu wissen, wer sie mitliest."* (Addresses shadow AI use in firms)
2. *"Ihr Datenschützer will einen Beleg nach § 203 StGB. Hier ist er."* (Addresses audit readiness)
3. *"Kein US-Server. Kein Training. Deutscher Gerichtsstand in Nürnberg."* (Addresses data residency & US cloud fear)
4. *"Vier Fragen. 60 Sekunden. Dann wissen Sie, ob Ihre Kanzlei-KI konform ist."* (Drives the Einstufung quiz)
5. *"Warum Standard-Cloud-Anbieter jede persönliche Haftungsübernahme für § 203 ablehnen."* (Highlights our unique legal differentiator)
6. *"Unterschriftsreife AVV- und § 203-Zusatzvereinbarungen für Ihre Kanzlei."* (Addresses legal paperwork friction)
7. *"SHA-256 Hash-Chaining: Der unveränderliche Nachweis für jede KI-Anfrage."* (Addresses audit opacity)
8. *"Automatisierter Art. 30 VVT-Eintrag pro KI-Anfrage statt Excel-Handarbeit."* (Saves administrative time)
9. *"Die 12 Punkte, die Ihr DSB prüft, bevor er KI in der Praxis freigibt."* (Lead magnet hook)
10. *"Getestet und für sicher befunden: KI-Einsatz unter Wahrung der Verschwiegenheitspflicht."* (Reassurance for liability insurance)

### Calendar
- **Day -14 to -8:** Finalize Meta ad creatives (per `ADS.md`), set up CAPI and Funnel API webhooks, verify instant form flows.
- **Day -7 to -1:** Soft launch with direct outreach to 20 friendly law and medical practices; test onboarding and manual key issuance.
- **Day 0 (Launch Day):** Go live with Meta Campaigns 1, 2, and 5 (broad targeting, instant form checklist, and compliance positioning). Publish announcement post on founder LinkedIn profiles.
- **Day 1 to 7:** Monitor ad set delivery and cost per lead (CPL). Ensure CAPI deduplication is running cleanly. Do not alter ad sets during the initial learning phase.
- **Day 8 to 14:** Review conversion rates from `FreeDownload` (Checklist) to `Lead` (Quiz / Access Request). Deploy secondary ad variations based on top-performing hooks.
- **Day 15 to 30:** Initiate outreach for professional media/newsletter placements. Review first month subscription conversions (€290/mo Compliance tier).

## 4. Budget & Unit Economics

### Budget Split (Starting at €150/Day / ~€4,500/Month)
- **Meta Ads (Instant Form & Einstufung):** €100/day (€3,000/month)
- **Professional Journal / Newsletter Placements:** €1,500/month

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) Cap
- **Compliance Tier Value:** €290/month = €3,480 LTV (assuming 12-month average retention).
- **Maximum Allowable CAC (LTV / 3):** Up to **€1,160** per acquired Compliance subscriber.
- **Target CAC (Blended):** Under **€350**, driven by low-friction instant form lead capture and high intent.

### Three Metrics to Watch Weekly
1. **Cost Per Lead (CPL) for Freigabe-Checkliste:** Target < €25 per instant form download.
2. **Quiz-to-Access Conversion Rate:** Percentage of visitors completing `/einstufung` who request an API key or dossier. Target > 15%.
3. **Compliance Trial-to-Paid Conversion Rate:** Percentage of free trial / dossier buyers upgrading to the €290/mo recurring subscription. Target > 20%.
