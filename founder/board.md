# Board Summary: konforme-ki.de

- **Vote:** 2 Fund If, 1 Fund (Average Score: 7.0/10) → **FUND IF**

## 1. Executive Summary & Verdict
The board evaluates **konforme-ki.de** as a highly focused, strategically sound positioning pivot targeting German professional secret holders (§ 203 StGB: law firms, tax advisors, medical practices, notaries). While generic sovereign cloud providers (IONOS, STACKIT, T-Systems) win on ISO/C5 certifications for general enterprise IT, their corporate structures and legal teams refuse to sign personal criminal liability addendums under § 203 StGB. konforme-ki.de owns this exact defensible wedge.

## 2. Top Risks Raised by Multiple Board Members
1. **Brand & Product Confusion:** Mixing generic developer inference (Router/Sidecar) with strict professional secrecy (§ 203 Deutschland-Inferenz) risks diluting the core value proposition and confusing buyers.
2. **Infrastructure & Legal Execution:** The promise of zero US involvement and personal criminal liability addenda must be airtight in production (resolving Cloudflare tunnel / routing dependencies and finalizing legal review of Anlage 1).
3. **Competitive Response:** Larger vertical legal-tech suites or agile competitors attempting to replicate the § 203 signing wrapper.

## 3. Merged Conditions (Checklist)
- [ ] **Strict Product Segmentation:** Keep § 203 Deutschland-Inferenz as the undisputed hero offering; relegate Router and Sidecar to secondary sections (`#weitere-wege`).
- [ ] **Production Infrastructure Audit:** Ensure all technical claims in the audit trail (`RekordKarte`, Nürnberg hardware, zero US subprocessor leakage) match live production reality.
- [ ] **Unassailable Legal & RDG Boundaries:** Maintain prominent disclaimers preventing any perception of formal legal advice while delivering uncompromised contractual and cryptographic compliance.
- [ ] **Streamlined Onboarding:** Minimize friction between free token testing, agreement review, and manual key issuance.

## 4. The Strongest Vision
konforme-ki.de is not a commodity GPU reseller trying to out-scale IONOS or T-Systems. It is the trusted compliance-first signing partner and cryptographic audit layer for German Berufsgeheimnisträger who cannot legally use standard cloud AI. By combining personal executive sign-off on server operations with immutable SHA-256 hash chains, it bridges the gap between strict professional secrecy laws and modern AI capabilities.
